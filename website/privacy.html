Privacy

What we collect, and what happens to it.

Last updated 14 August 2026

The short version

The form collects what you type into it and emails it to us. There are no cookies, no analytics, and no advertising trackers on this site. We don't sell anything to anyone. Ask us to delete your inquiry and we will.

What the form collects

Only what you enter: your preferred name and email, optionally a phone number, city and state, and how you'd rather be reached — plus your answers about the work you're looking for. Every question apart from name and email can be skipped.

What we ask you not to send

Please don't put Social Security numbers, ITINs, EINs, bank or routing numbers, or dates of birth into the form. It travels over the open internet and there's no attachment facility, by design. Anything like that comes later, through a channel meant for it.

Where it goes

The form is processed by Formspree, which passes it to us by email. On our plan Formspree keeps a copy for 30 days; after that the email in our inbox is the only record. Formspree handles the submission under its own privacy policy.

If you book a call

The optional booking button hands you to Calendly, which collects the details it needs to make the appointment — typically name, email and phone number — and puts it on our calendar. That happens under Calendly's own privacy policy. Booking is optional; we reply either way.

Your browser

While you're filling the form in, your answers are held in your own browser so a refresh doesn't lose them. They're cleared when you close the tab and are never sent anywhere until you press send. No cookies are set, and no analytics or tracking scripts run on this site.

Hosting and fonts

The site is served by GitHub Pages, which receives the ordinary request information any web server does, including your IP address. Type is loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser makes a request to Google to fetch it. Neither is something we control or read.

Keeping it and deleting it

We keep inquiries as long as they're useful for talking to you, and longer where a professional or legal obligation applies once you become a client. If you'd like your inquiry deleted before that, email us and we'll do it — you don't need to give a reason.

Sending the form isn't hiring us

Sending it starts a conversation and nothing more. SATC is engaged only once we both sign an engagement letter.

Asking us about any of this

Email us with a question, a correction, or a deletion request.
