Printer specification
Send with the artwork
Trim & bleed Artwork is drawn at trim: 3.5 × 2 in. Ask for 0.125 in bleed on all four sides — the navy back is full-bleed and needs it — and keep a 0.125 in safe margin inside trim. All type sits at least 0.22 in from the trim edge, so a 1 mm cutting drift is harmless.
Stock 16pt uncoated. Heavy dot gain is expected, which is why nothing on the card is set below 6pt — don't let anyone shrink it to fit more in.
Deliberately absent No mailing address and no CPA licence number on any card. Both by decision, not oversight — the printer should not add them.
Ink Navy #132437, oxblood #6A2833, gold #C0A265, shell #F7F7F5. Gold appears on the navy back only; it never carries a hairline or type that must survive a photocopy.
Checking this proof Printed at 100% on Letter, every card above is actual size — hold a ruler or an existing card against it. If the cards measure anything other than 3.5 × 2 in, the print scaling was wrong, not the file.
