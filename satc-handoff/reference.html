Who This Is For

Worth five minutes,
or worth skipping.

Before you fill anything in — here's who we're a good fit for, and who we honestly aren't.

Probably a fit

Send the form

  • Small businesses that want bookkeeping and tax handled in one place, by one person
  • Owners whose books have fallen behind and need catching up before anything else
  • Businesses filing in more than one state, or running more than one entity
  • Anyone who wants process and controls built, not just a return filed
  • Individuals with business, rental, or equity income that makes a return non‑trivial

Probably not a fit

We'll point you elsewhere

  • Companies needing a statutory financial‑statement audit — we don't do audit work
  • Anyone shopping purely for the cheapest one‑off return
  • Publicly traded companies and their subsidiaries
  • Anyone hoping for aggressive positions we couldn't defend in writing

Not sure which column you're in? Send the form anyway. We'll read it and tell you straight — and if we're not the right firm for the work, we'll say so rather than take the engagement.
