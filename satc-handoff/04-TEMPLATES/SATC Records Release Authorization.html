Goes out with the engagement letter, by default, to any client who had a previous accountant. The firm, 26 August 2026: "let's just make an attachment that we send for them to sign by default along with the engagement letter." It replaces the onboarding letter's paragraph about contacting the predecessor, which asked the client to authorize something in writing without giving them the writing.

The client signs this, not the firm. It is written in the client's voice, addressed to their former preparer, and the firm's name appears only as the place records go.

Deliberately not here

  1. No fee, no scope, no deadline. This is one instruction from a client to their former preparer. Everything else is in the letter it travels with.
  2. No claim about anyone's obligations. It asks; it does not tell the predecessor what any rule requires of them. What a former preparer must release, and on what terms, is between the client and that firm.
  3. No "we will contact them on your behalf". The firm does not need to: the client signs this and sends it, which is faster and needs no second authorization.
