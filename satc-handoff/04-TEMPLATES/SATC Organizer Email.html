What this is

The email a returning client gets in January with their organizer. It was a PDF cover letter until 24 October 2026, when the firm said it "is actually supposed to be sent as an email not an attachment". The words are the letter's, with what the change forces: no letterhead, address block, date or footer, and "is enclosed" became "is attached". The organizer itself is printed from Drake; this software does not have it, so the preparer attaches it before sending. Fields are in FIELDS - Organizer Email.md.
