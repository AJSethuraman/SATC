01What we need from you

If an item does not apply to you, tell us that rather than leaving it out — we cannot tell a missing document from one that does not exist, and we will keep asking.

02How to send it

We use Encyro for everything containing your personal or financial information. You will receive an invitation at dreyes@example.com; the link works without an account and the upload is encrypted in both directions.

Please do not email documents as ordinary attachments. Email is not secure, and a return has your Social Security number, your income, and your bank details in one file.

Scans or clear photographs are both fine. One document per file, named plainly, is easier for both of us than a single large bundle.

03Access we may ask for

Where the work requires it, we will ask for read-only access to an accounting system or a bank feed. Read-only means we can see the activity and cannot move money, initiate a payment, or change your records outside the system's audit trail.

We will never ask you for a password. Access is granted through the platform's own invitation, in your name, and you can revoke it at any time. If anyone contacts you claiming to be from this firm and asks for a password or a wire transfer, it is not us — call 307-941-0508 and ask.

04Your previous accountant

We will contact Halloran & Reeve CPAs for the prior-period records and returns we need. That request requires your written authorisation, which is included in the package for signature. We do not need you to explain the change, and we will not discuss it with them.

Send us your most recent filed return and the prior year's closing balances even if you expect them from the other firm. Having them from you means the work is not waiting on someone else's response time.

05What happens once we have everything

  • We review what arrived and confirm in writing that the file is complete, usually within three business days.
  • If something is missing or unclear, you get one consolidated list of questions rather than a trickle of emails.
  • Work begins when the file is complete. Our target for the first deliverable is April 1, 2027.
  • Everything for your signature comes through Encyro, and finished documents are delivered password-protected.

A file that arrives complete and reconciled moves faster and, because our estimate assumes it, usually costs what we quoted. Incomplete records are the single largest cause of both delay and a revised estimate.
