6 fields + 5 repeating lists + 9 flags.

Deliberately not here

  1. No attachments inside the email. The preparer attaches the revised returns; the email refers to them.
  2. No fee. The invoice owns it.
  3. No masthead or legal footer. This is an email from a person, signed with a first name, like the firm's other client emails.
  4. No password. The email says the password is unchanged and never carries any part of it.
