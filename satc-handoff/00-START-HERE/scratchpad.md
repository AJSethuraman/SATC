# SAT-C LLP — Proposal Deck

## Purpose
Client-facing. Shown in a first or second meeting to demonstrate the *range* of
what an engagement can include, so a prospect doesn't self-select out of work
they'd actually value. **No prices** — everything quoted per engagement.

## Title sequence (short topic noun-phrases throughout)

01 · Engagement Overview            — cover, navy
02 · Three Disciplines, One Desk    — what the firm does
03 · How Depth Scales               — the core idea: one relationship, three depths
04 · Foundation — Accounting        — depth 1
05 · Compliance — Tax               — depth 2
06 · Advisory                       — depth 3
07 · Scope Comparison               — the table; the centrepiece of the deck
08 · Reporting Cadence              — monthly / quarterly / annual, client's choice
09 · Engagement Fit                 — who this suits, who it doesn't
10 · Next Steps                     — what happens after this meeting
11 · Contact                        — navy, close

## Scope boundaries the deck must respect (from the client, 2nd pass)

- **Accounting is not monthly by default.** Interval is set jointly — monthly,
  quarterly or annual. Slides 02, 03, 04, 07 and 08 all say so now.
- **No invented deliverables.** "Cash-flow reporting against a rolling forecast"
  was cut; nothing in the deck promises a service that doesn't exist.
- **Credit and collections is ADVICE, not operations.** The firm does not run
  collections. Slide 06 says "recommendations on credit terms and collections."
- **Process documentation is ADVICE, not ownership.** The firm will not own a
  client's process or documentation. Slide 06's panel states the boundary
  outright: "Advisory only — we advise, you own and operate it."
- Slide 06 was retitled from "Process — Controls & Analytics" to "Advisory"
  precisely so the title itself can't imply delivery.

Read as a ToC: what we do → how it scales → each level → compare → how we work
→ whether we fit → what happens next. No punchline titles, no "it's not X it's Y".

## Layout system
- **Navy full-bleed:** 01, 11 (bookends). Nothing else — max 2 background colours.
- **Shell (#F7F7F5):** 02, 03, 04, 05, 06, 08, 09, 10
- **Paper (#FFF):** 07 only — the table earns the brightest ground.
- **Depth slides 04/05/06 are strictly parallel:** mono number top-left, title,
  short lede, 4-item list left, "What you get" panel right. Same positions.
- **Included-marker in the table is the brand square** (oxblood), not a tick —
  shape carries the meaning, so it survives greyscale and photocopy.
- Gold appears only as hairlines. Oxblood only as accent/marker. Navy dominant.

## Text discipline
Max 4 bullets per slide, max ~12 words each. The table is the only dense slide.
Slides 03 and 08 carry diagrams rather than prose.
