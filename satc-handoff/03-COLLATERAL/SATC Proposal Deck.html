How We Work

Reporting cadence is your choice.

Monthly

Closest view

  • Suits payroll, inventory or debt covenants
  • Statements within an agreed number of days
  • Problems surface while they are still fixable
Quarterly

Middle ground

  • Suits steady businesses without monthly pressure
  • Estimates calculated in the same pass
  • One review call each quarter
Annually

Lightest touch

  • Suits simple operations, or a first year
  • Books brought current once, for the year
  • Return prepared from the same file

Whichever you choose is written into the engagement letter, and can change as the business does. We will tell you if we think you have picked the wrong one.

Fit

Where we are the right firm.

A good fit
