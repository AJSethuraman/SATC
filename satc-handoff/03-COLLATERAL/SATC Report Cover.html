Basis of presentation

These statements were prepared from the accounting records of Clifton Millworks LLC and information supplied by its management. Management is responsible for the accuracy and completeness of that information and for the financial statements themselves.

Figures are presented in US dollars. Amounts in parentheses are deductions or credits. Comparative figures are shown for the prior period on the same basis. Where figures have been rounded, the rounding is noted on the schedule concerned.

The entity is taxed as a partnership; accordingly, no provision for federal income tax is included in these statements. Members' individual tax positions are addressed separately in the tax summary at section 05.

No assurance is provided on these financial statements. We have not audited, reviewed, or compiled them and express no opinion or conclusion on them.
