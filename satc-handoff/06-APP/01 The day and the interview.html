Job 01 — the day

What is waiting on you

Three groups, in this order, and the order is the whole design: things that stop until you act, things that are out with somebody else, things nobody has started. A client appears in the first group only if the software cannot move without you — a fact is missing, a sentence is unwritten, a check refuses. Job 02, continued

The last look, without thirty-seven buttons

Twenty-six facts and a Change button on every one. The word appears twenty-six times and does no work: you are not looking for the word, you are looking for the answer that is wrong. So the row is the control — hover or tab to it and change appears on that row alone. Any answer the software refused to invent is in the third colour, and the count of those is at the top, because that is what makes this screen worth reading before you press the button.
