Job 04 — the one that matters

The gate

Today the page leads with a bordered paragraph and then eleven rows of table, and the eye lands on the table because it is the biggest thing. That is backwards: the table is the evidence, and you only read evidence after you know the verdict. So:

  • The verdict, in the first inch, as a sentence with a number in it. Nothing above it but who the client is.
  • What is unresolved, as objects — one card each, saying which document, what the check read, and what you would do about it. This is the list the old page buried.
  • The override is a locked control. The reason comes first and the button will not enable until it is written. That is not friction for its own sake: the button’s whole purpose is to put a sentence in the file, so a button that can be pressed without one is a bug.
  • The eleven checks fold away, with their denominators on the summary line, so the evidence is one keystroke from the verdict and not on top of it.

Where I changed the wording: the failing check named a file — SAT-C Engagement Letter - Reyes - 2026.html. It now names the document and the section, because that is the thing you would go and open. And the check itself was called “no banned legalese and no British spelling”; banned is a word about the software’s rules, so it now reads “no word the firm has ruled out” — the firm ruled, not the linter.
