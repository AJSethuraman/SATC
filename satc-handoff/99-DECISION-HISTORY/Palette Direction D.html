Six tightening moves

01Oxblood does two jobs, not four. The italic LLP and the action button. Labels and the discipline line move to a warm grey #8A857E — instantly quieter.
02Navy deepens and cools from #16283C to #132437, opening a clear value gap above the oxblood so the two never compete.
03Gold appears exactly once — a 1px rule under the total, nowhere else. It's the last thing your eye lands on, which is the whole point.
04Wordmark tracking pulled to −0.022em at weight 500. Spectral at 400 was too airy for a firm's name; the disciplinary line goes small with wide 0.26em tracking to hold the counterweight.
05All figures to tabular mono. Columns align to the digit on an accounting document — this is the single biggest credibility gain in the sheet.
06Paper cooled to #F1EEE6 and hairlines lightened. The old cream pushed the oxblood muddy; a touch less yellow lets it stay a red.
