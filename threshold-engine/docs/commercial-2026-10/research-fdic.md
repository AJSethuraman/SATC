# FDIC BankFind API: commercial loan fields (research, 16 Oct 2026)

Read-only research. No repository file was edited. All amounts are **$ thousands**, as the FDIC reports them. "Q" means the FDIC's quarterly field; "YTD" means year-to-date.

## Sources and method

- **Definitions, two FDIC files, both downloaded 16 Oct 2026:**
  - `https://api.fdic.gov/banks/docs/risview_properties.yaml`: the financials endpoint's schema, 2,378 fields. It gives a title, plus a formula for each derived ratio. Only 88 of the 2,378 have a `description`.
  - `https://api.fdic.gov/banks/docs/All%20Financial%20Reports.xlsx`, sheet `Reference-Variables&Definitions`: 2,328 fields, each with a title and definition text. This file is linked from `https://api.fdic.gov/banks/docs/`.
  - (`banks.data.fdic.gov/docs/...` returns a 301 redirect.)
- **Data:** `https://api.fdic.gov/banks/financials`, index `risview_20260819185831`. The latest quarter is **2026-06-30**.
- **Each field was confirmed four ways.** It is in at least one definitions file, AND a live query returns values for it. For each field I recorded:
  1. the first, first non-zero and last quarter in which any filer reports it (`FIELD:[* TO *]`, sorted by REPDTE);
  2. the full history for KeyBank (cert 17534) and JPMorgan (cert 628), 1984Q1–2026Q2, 170 quarters each;
  3. industry sums over every filer at 2001Q4, 2006Q4, 2007Q1, 2009Q4, 2010Q1, 2024Q3, 2024Q4, 2025Q1 and 2026Q2;
  4. per-bank rows at 2007Q1–2008Q1, 2024Q3 and 2024Q4, used for identity checks.
- **Control: an unknown field name is silently dropped.** The API returns no error and no key. `NTRENREQ` and `LSNUS` both return 0 rows. So "absent" proves nothing, and a field is counted as confirmed only when it returns values.

### Net charge-off *rate* fields: what each one divides by (from the YAML formulas, checked against KeyBank 2026Q2)

- **`NT*QR` and `NT*R` (e.g. `NTCIQR`, `NTRENRSQR`, `NTRECONQR`, `NTREMULQR`, `NTREAGQR`, `NTAGQR`, `NTDEPQR`, `NTOTHQR`, `NTLSQR`) divide by average TOTAL ASSETS, not by the loan book.**
  - The Q versions use `ASSET2`; the YTD versions use `ASSET5`.
  - Check: `NTCIQR` 0.1119 = NTCIQ×4/ASSET2 exactly. The book-based rate would be 0.577.
  - Do not use these as loss rates.
- **Rates that divide by the loan book:**
  - `NTRECOQR` (construction, Q), `NTREMUQR` (multifamily, Q) and `NTRENRQR` (NFNR, Q) are RISVIEW-native and **rounded to 2 decimals**. Check: NTREMUQR 1.31 vs 1.3081 computed over a two-quarter average balance; NTRENRQR 0.04 vs 0.0449.
  - YTD annualized: `NTRECOSR` = NTRECOSA/LNRECON5, `NTREMULR`, `NTRENRSR`.
  - C&I: `IDNTCIQR` = **NTCIQ×4 / LNCI5**, where LNCI5 is the *year-to-date* average balance. KeyBank 2026Q2: 0.5857, against 0.577 over a two-quarter average. `IDNTCIR` is the YTD version.
  - "All other loans and leases (incl. farm)": `NTALLOTHQR` = (NTDEPQ+NTFORGVQ+NTOTHQ+NTLSQ+NTAGQ)×4/(LNOTCI2+LNAG22).
- **No book-based rate exists** for farmland, ag, depository, other loans or leases, nor for the construction and NFNR sub-splits. Compute those from the dollar fields.
- **Annualized dollar fields:** `NTCIQA` = NTCIQ×4 (209,544 = 52,386×4), plus `NTAGQA` and `NTREAGQA`.
- **The ratio fields are zero-filled back to 1984Q1, before their numerator exists.** For example, `NTRECONQR` has values from 1984Q1 although `NTRECONQ` starts in 1990Q1. "First non-null" is therefore meaningless for ratio fields.

### Do the Q fields equal the YTD differences?

Mostly. For KeyBank, DRxxQ = DRxx(q) − DRxx(q−1) in all but 0–30 of 426–510 quarters per category. The exceptions cluster in merger and restatement quarters: KeyBank 2004Q4 (DRCIQ 15,814 vs YTD difference 20,715) and 2016Q4; JPM 2019Q2 (DRCIQ 225,444 vs 278,000) and 2001Q4. **The FDIC's Q fields are FDIC-computed, not a naive YTD difference.** I did NOT CHECK how they are computed in those quarters.

## 2. Breaks found in the data

**A. 2007Q1: construction and nonfarm-nonres splits begin.** Every split field (`LNRECNFM`, `LNRECNOT`, `LNRENROW`, `LNRENROT` and their DR/CR/NT/P3/P9/NA) is null for every filer through 2006Q4 and populated from 2007Q1. The totals are continuous across the break:

| Industry | 2006Q4 | 2007Q1 |
|---|---|---|
| `LNRECONS` | 565,299,141 | 582,287,984 = `LNRECNFM` 196,759,376 + `LNRECNOT` 377,103,623 + 8.4bn not split |
| `LNRENRES` | 904,353,451 | 905,614,197; `LNRENROW` 341,862,170 + `LNRENROT` 479,548,876 (7,895 filers) |

- KeyBank 2007Q1: LNRECNFM 884,562 + LNRECNOT 7,633,182 = LNRECONS 8,517,744. LNRENROW 4,278,597 + LNRENROT 4,495,612 = LNRENRES 8,774,209.
- JPM 2007Q1: LNRENROW 9,642,000, LNRENROT 6,648,000.
- **Coverage trap 1: the splits are not complete industry-wide until 2008Q1.**
  - In 2007Q1–2007Q4, about 2,370–2,510 FFIEC 041 filers with construction loans greater than 0 report the construction split as 0/0. That is 8.4bn at 2007Q1 and 13.1bn at 2007Q4. They are small banks (largest $0.57bn of assets at 2007Q1).
  - About 2,925–3,044 041 filers report the NFNR split as 0/0 in the same quarters.
  - Both drop to zero banks at 2008Q1. That matches the FDIC's own note on `LNRENROW`: *"Prior to March 2008, listed as a memoranda item for Call report filers that met specific criteria."*
  - So for an industry series, the split begins 2008Q1, or the series must exclude the zero-filled banks.
- **Coverage trap 2: TFR filers (CALLFORM 100) report the construction split, but the NFNR owner-occupied split is null.**
  - That is 838 filers and 64.1bn of NFNR at 2007Q1, rising to 77.4bn at 2008Q1. It lasts until TFR reporting ended in Dec 2011.
  - The FDIC definition says the split is "considered confidential for TFR filers".
  - 17 filers with CALLFORM 2 still lack every split at 2024Q3 and 2024Q4 (3.5bn construction, 8.4bn NFNR).

**B. NDFI loans first appear as a separate item in 2010Q1** (`LNNDEPD`, domestic offices). Any filer, KeyBank and JPM all start then; the industry sum at 2010Q1 is 56,279,046.

- Before 2010, NDFI loans are inside `LNOTHER`, whose YAML title still says "LN TO NONDEP FIN INST & OTH LN".
- At the 2009Q4→2010Q1 boundary:
  - `LNCI` 1,226,606,209 → 1,192,325,997
  - `LNOTHER` 206,099,430 → 248,020,531
  - `LNCON` 1,057,804,377 → 1,366,188,951 (the consumer jump is the same quarter; its cause was NOT CHECKED).

**D. Depository-institution charge-offs end at 2016Q4.** `DRDEP`, `CRDEP`, `NTDEP` and their Q versions are null for every filer from 2017Q1. `NTDEPQR` continues as zeros. Balances, P3, P9 and NA continue. NOT CHECKED: where those charge-offs are reported after 2016 (presumably inside "all other").

**E. Older breaks, for completeness:**

- 30-89 day past dues for every category start in 2001Q1.
- RE charge-off flows start in 1990Q1 (KeyBank and JPM in 1991Q1).
- `LNACOTH` (acceptances of other banks) ends 2001Q1.
- Definitions note that before 2001, banks under $300m reported "all other loans" inside C&I charge-offs (`NTCI`, `DRCIQ`).

## 4. credit-suite fields re-verified (`credit-suite/src/credit_suite/sources/fdic/fields.py`)

- **Re-verified: all present in the YAML and returning data for KeyBank and JPM through 2026Q2.**
  - Balances: `LNRECONS`, `LNRENRES`, `LNREMULT`, `LNCI`.
  - Past due and nonaccrual: `P3CI`, `P9CI`, `NACI`, `P3RECONS`, `P9RECONS`, `NARECONS`, `P3RENRES`, `P9RENRES`, `NARENRES`, `P3REMULT`, `P9REMULT`, `NAREMULT`.
  - Quarterly net charge-offs: `NTRECONQ`, `NTREMULQ`, `NTCIQ`.
  - NT = DR − CR holds in every quarter for all of them.
- **`NTRENREQ` is not an FDIC field** (it is in neither definitions file, and the API returns 0 rows). credit-suite already records it as never returned.
  - But its comments say *"the FDIC publishes no quarterly variant"* (`consistency.py:37`, `provenance_seed.py:244`). **That is wrong.**
  - The quarterly net field exists as **`NTRENRSQ`**. The YAML title is "NONFARM NONRES RE LN NET-CHG-QTR"; the xlsx says "(Qtly, $) Net amount of nonfarm nonresidential properties … charged-off".
  - It has data from 1990Q1. KeyBank 2026Q2: 1,004 = `DRRENRSQ` 1,289 − `CRRENRSQ` 285.
  - The name was guessed as an "8-char truncation" of the wrong stem.
  - This is a likely fix for credit-suite. I did not make it, because the task is read-only.
- **C&I scope:** `LNCI`, `NTCIQ` and `P3CI` are total C&I by the FDIC definitions. The non-U.S. part is published separately (`LNCINUS`, `NTCINUSQ`, `P3CINUS`).

## 5. Pitfalls for whoever builds this

- An unknown field is silently dropped. Assert that every requested key comes back for at least one filer.
- The xlsx's "(YTD, $)" / "(Qtly, %)" tags are unreliable:
  - `LNCI` is tagged YTD but is a quarter-end stock.
  - `LNOTHER` is tagged "%" but is in dollars.
  - The definitions for `NTALLOTHQR` and `NTALLOTHR` describe noncontrolling-interest income, which is plainly a copy error. The YAML formula is authoritative for those.
- Class NCO ratios over *assets* look like loss rates and are not.
- Ag delinquency, nonaccrual and charge-offs are split between 031 filers (`*AG`) and 041/051 filers (`*AGSM`). The two sets of filers do not overlap at 2026Q2.
  - The FDIC titles the `*SM` items "(Included Above)". Which line they are included in is NOT CHECKED.
  - The `P3AGSM` definition: banks under $300m report it only if ag production loans exceed 5% of total loans.
  - Summing `*AG` alone covers 80 banks.
- `LNNDEPD` is domestic offices; `LNOTHER` and `LNCI` are consolidated. For banks with foreign offices (JPM), "other excluding NDFI" should use `LNNDEPC` from 2024Q4. There is no consolidated NDFI field before 2024Q4.

## 6. What I could not confirm

- **NDFI charge-offs, past dues and nonaccruals:** COULD NOT. Neither definitions file has such a field, so the API does not carry them. Whether the Call Report form itself has them after Dec 2024 is NOT CHECKED.
- **Post-Dec-2024 NDFI sub-categories:** COULD NOT. They are not in the API.
- **Leases split into consumer vs commercial** (charge-offs, past dues or balances): COULD NOT. The API has only `LS` and its foreign/non-U.S. variants, and `LSNUS` is in the xlsx but not served. The form's RC-C 10.a/10.b split is NOT CHECKED here.
- **RC-L commercial-line or C&I commitments as a separate field:** COULD NOT. They exist only inside `UCOTHER`, per its definition.
- **Domestic C&I** is not a published field. It can be computed as LNCI − LNCINUS (by definition; not tied to filed RCON/RCFD codes).
- **Causes of the 2010Q1 and 2024Q4 reclassifications, and where depository charge-offs went after 2016:** NOT CHECKED against FFIEC revision notices. The observed numbers are reported above.
- **How the FDIC computes Q fields in merger/restatement quarters:** NOT CHECKED. Mismatches are listed in §1.
- **Industry first dates** are "any filer reports the field". A field is zero-filled where the bank was not required to report it (2007 041 filers, the pre-data ratio fields), so I give the first non-zero date as well. A sum across filers at 2024Q4 exceeds `LNLSGR` by 0.02%; not investigated.
- **Tie-out to filed Call Reports (FFIEC CDR)** for the new fields: NOT CHECKED. Confirmation here is against the FDIC definitions plus live API values, plus internal identities (category sum = LNLSGR, NT = DR − CR).
