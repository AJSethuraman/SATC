Did not fit, and what I did instead
An axis that refits to the quarters and still ignores a spike. Bounds cannot follow a cell. Instead the plotted values are cut by formula at each series' fixed 1st and 99th percentile, so Excel's auto axis fits what is left.
A shaded normal range on the Dashboard. Its charts are scatter (years as decimals); an area band needs a category axis. Kept: two stone dashed lines, one legend entry. The series tabs keep the band.
Year labels that suit any window. The major unit is fixed when the file is built: 5 years (big charts), 10 (small). A window under 5 years shows one label or none; the readings table and the boxes still say the quarters.
A search box for 76 tabs. No macros, no text boxes. The Overview and Usable with care are the index; tab colour groups the bar.
The whole Overview on one screen. 41 rows plus 5 sections at 15 pt need 980 px; the screen has 780. Frozen header and names instead; the fold falls in Business.
Series names as links in the Dashboard table. HYPERLINK is not in the 2007 list the brief allows.
