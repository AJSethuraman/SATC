1 · How the dots connect

The same fact travels two roads. Only the lower one is the check: it reads the 10-Q document the reader sees, fresh from SEC, with its own parser, so a fault in the build's pull or parser would show as a difference. Both roads start at a document neither the bank's analyst nor this tool controls.

4 · How to run it yourself

From threshold-engine, with Python 3.9 or later and Excel. Put your own name and email in place of the contact (SEC refuses requests without one; it is not stored).

python -m threshold_engine peers classes-pull --cache books/sec_cache --classes config/peer-loan-classes.json --quarter 2026Q2 --user-agent "Your Name you@yourbank.com"
python -m threshold_engine book books/consumer-public.json consumer-credit.xlsx
cd docs/tie-out/peer-loan-classes-2026q2
powershell -File read_workbook.ps1 -Path ..\..\..\consumer-credit.xlsx -Out out\ours.json
set SEC_USER_AGENT=Your Name you@yourbank.com
python filed.py ../../../config/peer-loan-classes.json out/filed.json
python compare.py ../../../config/peer-loan-classes.json out/ours.json out/filed.json out/roster.json

The last prints "Tied out: N of 33" and every row that is not TIED, with why. To check one figure by hand: open the 10-Q link above, find the table in the photograph, and read the Education row's Total.

5 · What it found

  • The filings settle who holds student loans. Citizens (Education, $8,044m) and SoFi ($16,921m, at fair value) carry them as classes of their own; PNC folded education loans into Other consumer on 1 Jan 2026 "based on materiality considerations", with no amount; KeyCorp holds $182m in discontinued operations, outside loans, and names student loan refinancing among its products. Capital One's 10-Q says Discover "sold its student loan portfolio" before the merger closed. The word "student" does not appear at all in the other twelve 10-Qs (table below).
  • One bank is a clean comparison for unsecured lending: Capital One (Personal loans, $8,619m, its own class; its Call Report other consumer is these loans). Fifth Third splits out its unsecured loans ($626m, Table 44) and its solar loans ($4,314m, counted as unsecured by the analyst's ruling), but together they are about half its Call Report other consumer ($10.2bn average); the rest is RV and marine, other secured and point-of-sale, so its rate is not an unsecured rate. Every other bank's other consumer mixes unsecured lending with student, secured or undefined lending, or is mostly something else (M&T's recreational finance, Wells Fargo's and Bank of America's securities-based lending, SoFi's fair-value loans).
  • The readers missed two sentences, and this tie-out found them. The three agents that read the filings recorded "not stated" for KeyCorp's secured non-auto lending and Capital One's student loans. The tie-out's own search of the filed text found "Our consumer loans have collateral that includes residential real estate, automobiles, boats, and RVs." (KeyCorp) and the Discover sentence (Capital One). Both findings were corrected before this build.

6 · What I got wrong

  • The first comparison showed 3 DIFFERS: Fifth Third's RV and marine and Bank of America's two sentence figures. The fault was in the comparison script, not the workbook: it multiplied "$2.8 billion" by the whole amount instead of by $1,000 million a billion. Corrected; the workbook did not change.
  • The first photograph of the Citizens table came out blank: the 10-Q does not render scrolled in headless Chrome. The table is now cut out of the filed document and rendered alone, with its own styling.
  • I first marked Fifth Third a clean comparison for unsecured lending. That held for its balance split, not its loss rate: its Call Report other consumer is $10.2bn, of which $626m is unsecured. Corrected to Mixed.
  • Two findings the readers recorded as "not stated" were wrong (section 5): I had taken their records as the record. Every quote and every "not stated" is now checked against the filed text by the tie-out itself.

7 · What this does not prove

  • What a class is counted as is a judgment. The amounts tie to the filings; "counted as" does not tie to anything. Where a filing does not define a class (KeyCorp's Other consumer loans, Truist's Other consumer, M&T's Other, U.S. Bank's Installment), its contents are unknown and the tab says Mixed.
  • "Not stated" is a search, not a zero. It means the terms named were looked for in the 10-Q and not found. A bank can hold a small book it does not mention.
  • Holding company, not bank. The 10-Q is the holding company's; the Call Report's other consumer is the bank's. For consumer loans they are close but they were not reconciled here.
  • A printed figure is matched by its row's label and number, not by the table's title: a same-labeled row elsewhere in the filing carrying the same number would also pass. Each matched row is quoted in the roster so a reader can see which.
  • One quarter. The tab is for 30 June 2026; next quarter's filings need the pull run again, and a bank that changes its classes needs its entry in the file changed.
