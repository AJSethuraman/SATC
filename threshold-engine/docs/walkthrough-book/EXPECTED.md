# Expected answers: the book walkthrough

From `book.json` and the series files the build wrote, through `scale.py`. 105 series, 35 products and measures.

## B1 · Open the workbook as built

It opens on Summary; the tabs in order; two slicers, each with one button picked; Decide shows the first product and measure, no source picked and nothing decided.

10 reads.

## B2 · Read Summary, and every source's own lines on Lines by source

Every series' latest value, and Pick a source where its rating goes: 105 series, each with its own lines on Lines by source.

1235 reads.

## B3 · On Decide, click Credit card and Nonaccrual, and pick KeyBank under Lines set from

One row shows; the lines are KeyBank's own; every source is rated on them, beside what its own history would give.

52 reads.

## B4 · Type 3.5 under How rare High is (z), in the yellow row

High's line moves for every source of the row; Thresholds and Summary follow.

48 reads.

## B5 · Leave out 2012Q3 to 2013Q4, the period Decide suggests, with a reason

KeyBank's lines move as the reference says; the other sources' own lines do not; Assess lists the period under KeyBank's series, its Status whole.

38 reads.

3 reads.
