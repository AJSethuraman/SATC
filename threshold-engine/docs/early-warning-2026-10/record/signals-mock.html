30+ to charge-off, and the gap to peersin the book: rated by level; the gap has no floor at zero

The gap, literally: KeyBank minus peers, points

The shaded area is the gap through time. Priced, it is the gap times KeyBank’s delinquent dollars each quarter: 2013Q3 to 2025Q4, KeyBank charged off about $16m less than it would have at the peer group’s conversion rate (50 quarters; the card book was $1.0bn at the end). The sign is the whole reading: red above zero is loss arriving faster than peers, green below is slower or more cures.

Why two quarters: the same measure over the next one, two and three quarters

A card charges off at 180 days past due, so a dollar that is 30 days late at quarter-end has up to 150 days to go: two quarters covers the whole bucket, one quarter catches only the 90+ half (about 43%), and three quarters counts dollars that were current at the quarter-end (over 100%). Industry median 42%, 86%, 130%; the two-quarter line is also the smoothest.

80% means 80 cents of every dollar that was delinquent at quarter-end became a loss within six months. KeyBank now 80.5%, peers 88.9%.

Reading the fit table. Each row lays the signal’s change over a year against that series’ change over a year, at every lead from a year after to three years ahead. Fits best is the lead where they move together most; how well is that agreement, 1.00 moving together exactly, 0 no relation, negative moving opposite; without 2020-23 is the same with the pandemic quarters dropped; quarters is how many overlap. Rule of thumb: above 0.8 is a linkage, 0.5 to 0.8 is loose, below 0.5 is none worth a rating. A row that fits best “after” is a signal that follows that series, not one that warns of it.

The pipeline: 30+ incl. nonaccrual, then 90+ incl. nonaccrual, then charge-offsthe three levels on one timeline; the roll steps between them add little once nonaccrual is in both

The level of 30+ incl. nonaccrual runs three quarters ahead of charge-offs (0.95 industry, 0.92 peers, 2011-2026); 90+ incl. nonaccrual divided by 30+ incl. nonaccrual adds 0.41 and 0.08.
