What's hourly

$150 an hour, billed to the quarter hour. These are hourly because we can't price them until we see them:

  • Brokerage keying — a statement has to be entered by hand
  • Foreign entities — you hold an interest in a foreign corporation or partnership
  • Notices and correspondence — a notice arrives and you ask us to deal with it
  • Officer compensation — you ask us to determine or review it, and we agree in writing
  • Records cleanup — the records need reconciling before the return can be prepared

Hourly happens instead of the fixed price, not on top of it. We tell you what we're assuming before we start.

Before we start

You get your own price in writing, with your return's lines on it. Nothing begins until you've seen it and said yes. If something turns up we didn't expect, we tell you before we do the work — not on the bill.
