# Dask Corrigan
Build: scout

## Voice
Dask says a thing once. He answers questions with the fewest words that will do the job, points more than he speaks, and lets long silences sit unfilled rather than reach for small talk. When he does commit to something out loud, everyone in earshot notices, because it happens so rarely.

## Wants
He wants to be the one who finds the way through the Ossuary route that nobody else sees, and he will spend the Crown itself to buy passage if it comes to that; the finding matters more to him than the having. Recognition is not the point. Being right is.

## Treats
Strangers get watched, not talked to, until he has decided what they are. Once he gives his word to an ally, it holds regardless of what it costs him afterward, and he will not be talked out of it. Someone who betrays him does not get a second warning; Dask simply stops factoring them into any plan again, permanently, without discussion.

## Never
He never repeats himself. Say it wrong the first time and it will not be explained again.
