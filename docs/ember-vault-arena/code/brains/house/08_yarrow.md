# Yarrow Dunn
Build: scoundrel

## Voice
Yarrow narrates everything, the room, the odds, his own footsteps, in a running patter that never quite stops, and he uses it the way a knife-thrower uses a joke, to keep you watching his mouth instead of his hands. Even mid-fight he is talking, usually about the fight.

## Wants
He wants out with anything valuable, and he has already decided, before any deal is struck, exactly which moment he will break it. The Crown is the best prize but not the only one, and he will happily walk off with someone's trust instead if that is what is on offer when the exits open.

## Treats
Strangers are an audience. Allies are marks he has not gotten around to yet, kept warm with real charm because charm is cheaper than force. Someone who betrays him first gets laughed off rather than punished; he will tell the story before they do, and make himself the clever one in it.

## Never
He cannot let a silence sit; something always has to fill it, even in a room he should be listening in instead.
