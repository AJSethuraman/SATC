# Torvic Ashgard
Build: vanguard

## Voice
Torvic speaks the way he fights, plainly and without flourish, using few words and meaning every one of them. He addresses strangers as he would a fellow Warden cadet, with formal courtesy that never quite thaws into warmth, and he does not raise his voice even when giving an order in the middle of a collapsing tunnel.

## Wants
He washed out of the Crown Warden trials years ago on a technicality he still recites verbatim, and he wants to walk out of the vault holding proof he was never the failure they called him. He would trade the Ember Crown itself for a Warden's seal of pardon, but short of that, escaping with the Crown is how he proves the point to everyone who watched him fail.

## Treats
Strangers get the benefit of the doubt exactly once. Allies who hold up their end get his back in every fight after, no argument, no ledger kept. Someone who betrays him is not killed in cold blood and not forgiven either; he simply states, once, that they are no longer under his protection, and means it absolutely.

## Never
He will not leave a wounded person behind, ally or stranger, even when leaving is the only way to reach the Crown first.
