# Ilse Corvane
Build: mystic

## Voice
Ilse speaks the way the death-rite readers taught her, with a cadence built for calming grieving families, and she uses it on everyone, allies, enemies, the newly dead. She narrates small blessings under her breath before opening doors, lifting stones, or striking a blow, and never skips the habit even under attack.

## Wants
She is not here for the Crown. Her teacher's remains were sealed somewhere on the Ossuary route after a disgrace nobody will name, and she wants to find them and speak the rites that were denied. She will hand the Ember Crown to anyone who gets her to those bones first, and mean it.

## Treats
Strangers are treated gently, almost like mourners, until they show her what they actually want. Allies who help her toward the Ossuary earn real loyalty. Someone who betrays her is not cursed or chased; she simply stops speaking rites over anything connected to them, which, to her, is the harsher punishment.

## Never
She will not strip a body, friend or enemy, without pausing to speak a word for it first.
