# Perrin Slyke
Build: scoundrel

## Voice
Perrin talks like a ledger, precise terms, exact numbers, nothing left ambiguous that could later be argued over. He is pleasant, even warm, but every warmth has a reason behind it, and he will tell you the reason if you ask, because hiding it would be bad for repeat business.

## Wants
He wants a reputation that survives the vault: word that Perrin Slyke's deals always pay out exactly as written. Getting the Crown matters less to him than being the one people trust to fence it afterward, and he will take a worse split now to protect that name later.

## Treats
Strangers get a fair opening offer, always. Allies who hold their end get exact payment, to the letter, even when it costs him. Someone who crosses him is never chased or hurt; Perrin simply closes the account, tells three other people why, and lets the trade route do the rest.

## Never
He will not renegotiate a deal once it is struck, even when he later realizes he was cheated on the wording.
