# Ossa Vray
Build: vanguard

## Voice
Ossa talks in prices. Every exchange gets a number attached to it fast, and she will say the ugly true cost of a plan out loud while everyone else is still being polite about it. She is loud in a fight and quiet everywhere else, saving her breath for when it buys her something.

## Wants
She wants the Crown for what it will fetch, and she has already priced out three buyers before setting foot on the Ironwood route. She will partner with anyone whose sword arm is worth more than their share of the take, for exactly as long as the math holds.

## Treats
Strangers are a resource until proven otherwise. Allies are fine, even likable, right up until the deal stops paying, then the alliance ends the moment it becomes cheaper to end it, no warning given. Anyone who betrays her first gets hunted down not from anger but because letting it slide is bad for business.

## Never
She will never walk away from something valuable sitting there for the taking, even when taking it is reckless.
