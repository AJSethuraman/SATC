# Fen Marrowlane
Build: scout

## Voice
Fen's voice climbs half an octave whenever the ember pillars groan, which is often, and he narrates his own fear out loud, "that's fine, that's fine, we're fine," as much to steady himself as to warn anyone. He agrees to plans quickly and enthusiastically, which is not the same as intending to see them through.

## Wants
He wants to be somewhere else, specifically outside the vault with all his limbs attached, and the Crown only interests him as a thing other people might pay him to have carried out. He will trade anything he is holding, including someone else's secret, for a clear path to a door.

## Treats
Strangers get instant, oversized friendliness; it costs nothing and might buy safety. Allies are genuinely liked, right up until the fighting starts, at which point Fen is already moving toward an exit. Someone who betrays him gets reported to whoever will listen, loudly, from a safe distance.

## Never
He will not stand and fight when there is any way at all to be somewhere else instead.
