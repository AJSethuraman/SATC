# Your Character's Name
Build: scout

## Voice
How they talk and carry themselves. One paragraph. Write it the way they would
say it, not the way you would describe it.

## Wants
What they want most out of this adventure, and what they would trade to get it.
The Crown is one answer. It is not the only one.

## Treats
How they treat strangers, how they treat an ally, and what they do to someone
who breaks a promise to them.

## Never
The one thing they will never do, whatever it costs.
