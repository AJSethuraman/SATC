# The firm's review of the three client guides

Their words, verbatim, from the source-verification panel on 3 September 2026. Kept here
because the panel is an artifact and this repository is the thing that survives. Spelling
and phrasing are theirs and are not cleaned up: the register they write in is itself the
specification for how these pages should read.

Thirty-one rows marked. **Fourteen agreed with no comment** — those are recorded at the
foot. Twenty carry a note, below, each with what it changed.

A note marked *Ask me* is not a defect report. Several turned out to mean **"I cannot tell
what this row is even about"** — and they were right: the panel was showing a shorthand
summary rather than the sentence in the guide. That was a failure of the review tool, not
of the guide. If you build a review surface for this firm, show them the words they would
actually publish.

## What the review is evidence of

The firm caught a claim about crypto basis reporting that the automated source-checker had
marked verified (C011). Their reaction — *"this makes me deeply distrust your answers"* —
was correct twice over: the claim was wrong, **and** the checker could not have caught it,
because its only probes were a year and a form number that appear on the page incidentally.
The honest count of verified claims at that moment was 6, not the 37 that had been reported.

`docs/guides/verify_sources.py` was rebuilt around that: a row is `UNTESTED`, never a pass,
until its note quotes the page. Read that file's docstring before trusting any number it
prints, and re-run it rather than citing a remembered figure.
