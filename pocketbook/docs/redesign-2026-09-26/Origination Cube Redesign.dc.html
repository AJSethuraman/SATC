Redesign · launcher + workbook

Origination Cube

The launcher decides what gets run: the extract, what you're running, and which tests. The workbook holds the professional calls and the results. In the workbook, every setting sits in one of two blocks: Changes now or Needs a Run. Fifteen tabs become eleven. Each tab explains its method once, in a note you can fold away. Each pocket gets two separate verdicts: Worse? and Material?

Launcher: one place to choose tests

Choose tests handles both modes. For the bleed, you tick which columns to cut into bands, segment by and split by. For new variables, you pick the outcome, a shortlist of inputs, and columns to hold fixed. A launcher can show radio buttons and checkboxes properly; a sheet can't. What you're running, the saved-shortlist file and the column-recognition rules moved here too, because they're needed before the workbook exists.

Control: Changes now / Needs a Run

Two blocks with different header bands and different cell fills. You can tell which is which from the look of the cell before you read the label. Any Needs-a-Run change that differs from the last Run is marked "Waiting for a Run" and counted on Start here. The Materiality tab is folded in as a live preview beside its setting, because that's the only place it's used.

Merged tabs

Columns now includes Odd values and Learned, so you answer and forget on the column's own row. Pockets is Where it bleeds plus Three-way: they had the same columns, so slicers now switch the measure and the kind of pocket. Grids now includes Prevalence: counts beside the heat maps, one grid at a time. Record is Check plus Log.

New: New variables · Start here findings

New variables shows the second mode's results. Each input is shown found and then confirmed on held-back loans, with and without the held-fixed columns. Start here now also shows the headline finding, so you can see what the last Run found without opening a results tab. Tab colours: red for tabs you fill in, black for results, grey for the record.

PocketsWhich pockets lose more than their share, how much, and whether it's real and big enough to matter.
−
How this tab works · rows 3–10 fold away with −
A pocketOne band of one column (FICO 496–619) crossed with one segment (Broker). With Split, each pocket is halved at its own median REV_DEBT. Rest of bandEvery other loan in the same band. It decides the gap, the excess and both verdicts. The pocket against the whole book is on Grids. ExcessWhat the pocket lost above what it would have lost at the rest of its band's rate: in loans, or in dollars. For profit, the dollars it kept short of its band. Worse?Yes: at least 1.25× the rest of its band, and at most a 5% chance the gap is luck. Not sure: the gap is there, but it could be luck. Too few losses: under 10 losses, so no test. Chance it's luckHow often a gap this big would show if the pocket were no different, after allowing for testing many pockets at once. Bad loans: a standard two-sided test (the exact test under 30 loans). Dollar measures: loans shuffled 10,000 times within the band. Material?Yes when the excess reaches Control's materiality line. It's judged separately from Worse?: a pocket can be one without the other. Could have caughtThe smallest gap this pocket's size would catch 80% of the time. A "No" on a small pocket means little. Holds FICO fixed?Split only. REV_DEBT moves with FICO, so a split pocket in an ORIG_BAL grid may be showing FICO. Those sort last and aren't shaded.

The splitInside each pocket the loans are sorted by REV_DEBT and cut at that pocket's own median. Band and segment are the same on both sides, so the grid holds them fixed. How this tab works · fold away with −
Found, then confirmedThe loans are dealt at random, the same way every Run: 70% to find on and 30% held back. A candidate is compared on the found loans, then the same comparison is repeated once on the held-back loans. The comparisonNumber columns: high half against low half. Categories: the worst value on the found loans (Broker) against the rest. That choice is fixed before the held-back loans are looked at. Holds up?Yes when the held-back loans show the gap in the same direction at 95% sure. Only the held-back result counts: the found result is where the idea came from. FICO held fixedThe held-back comparison is repeated inside each FICO band and combined. If the gap disappears, the candidate was mostly telling you FICO. Material?Charge-offs above their share in the high half (or the worst value), scaled to the whole book, against Control's line. Written shortlistWhen the run confirms a shortlist saved in a file, the Found columns are left out and the file's name appears on Record.
