# Subjects — what brings this desk into play

`Answered from <source id>` is what makes routing deterministic AND what makes a
citation checkable: it names the subjects that bring a desk into play, and which
source answers each of them. Their union is what routing fires on, so there is no
second list to drift. It matches **whole words only** — substring matching once
made *"extension"* fire on *"extensive"*.

**It under-fires, and that is worth knowing.** The list matches a question's
subject, not its shape, so an inflected form is missed: `lease` does not fire on
`leasing`. Both are listed rather than the rule being loosened.

**Read the split before adding to it.** S1 answers *what the property is and how
much of its use is business*. S2 answers *paying a person back for a vehicle the
business does not own*. S3 answers *what proof § 274(d) demands*. S4 answers *how
the deduction is computed* — mileage versus actual, switching, leases — and it is
secondary, so everything routed there is a choice the firm makes rather than one
the desk settles.
