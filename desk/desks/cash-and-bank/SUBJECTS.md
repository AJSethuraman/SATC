# Subjects — what brings this desk into play

`Answered from <source id>` is what makes routing deterministic AND what makes a
citation checkable: it names the subjects that bring a desk into play, and which
source answers each of them. Their union is what routing fires on, so there is no
second list to drift. It matches **whole words only** — substring matching once made
*"extension"* fire on *"extensive"*.

**It under-fires, and that is worth knowing.** The list matches a question's
subject, not its shape, so an inflected form is missed: `defer` does not fire on
`deferring`. Add the inflections that matter rather than loosening the rule.

## cash-and-bank · When a difference between the books and the bank is a reconciling item rather than an entry in the books

**Answered from S1:** 1.446-1, method of accounting, clearly reflect income

**Answered from S2:** cash, bank, bank statement, reconcile, reconciled, reconciles, reconciling, reconciliation, outstanding check, outstanding checks, deposit in transit, deposits in transit, uncleared, cleared, checkbook, service charge, bank charge, petty cash

**Answered by `IRS Pub. 583 (12/2024), "Reconciling the checking account" — what the statement did not yet include`:** outstanding check, outstanding checks, deposit in transit, deposits in transit, uncleared, cleared

**Answered by `IRS Pub. 583 (12/2024), "Reconciling the checking account" — what the books are updated for`:** service charge, bank charge

---

**The two lines above are a NARROWING, and they are here because of a measured
hole.** `Answered from S2` says the publication answers bank reconciliation. It
cannot say WHICH PARAGRAPH OF IT, and this section states two rules with opposite
answers — what the statement did not yet include, and what the books are updated
for. On 5 September 2026 `serve()` was handed CB4's facts, a service charge
nobody entered, together with the TIMING citation, and returned *"a reconciling
item, no entry in the books"* — the opposite treatment — stamped
`checked_subject=True`. Real source, wrong paragraph.

A narrowing only ever removes: a subject nobody declares here is gated exactly as
before, so a desk that writes none of these lines behaves exactly as it did.
