No note. `capitalization_rule` is now on the capitalization desk's `Records:`
line, which closes the finding the firm themselves raised on the fourth docket:
*"What if this mattered only sometimes and we never even made a field for it."*

`no_field_for_this_fact` — the desk asked whether this client is treated

`context_not_on_file`: there is somewhere, and this client's file has not got

### 6 · The payment-rail position — held, and the note is the sharper criticism

> *"ok right now this entire thing sounds like a ton of just AI dribble, what
> in the world are you communicating with some of these"*

**Recorded verbatim rather than softened.** The position read:

> *where a payment for services shows no evidence it settled through a third
> party payment network, treat it as outside section 6050W and count it toward
> the $2,000 threshold*

Tenet S35 and the repository's own rule on client-facing register both say the
same thing and neither was applied: that sentence was written to satisfy a
requirement, not to be read. It is four pieces of jargon deep before it says
anything, and it never says the thing in words the reader brought with them.

The plain version is roughly: *if we cannot tell whether a payment went through
a card processor, assume it did not, and count it.* Whether that is the right
rule is a separate question the firm has not answered.

**No rewrite is proposed here.** The note is a complaint about the writing, not
an instruction to reword, and inventing an amendment they did not ask for is
the failure this whole store exists to prevent.
