# Origination Cube tenets

Standing rules for this project, in the firm's words, each dated. A tenet binds every tab,
report and deliverable the cube produces. Add one only on the firm's say-so.

## T1 · Method is said once, in one place, never beside every row

The firm, 26 Sep 2026:

> *"If there is a methodology note it should be summarized in one spot on a tab or whatever
> the deliverable includes. i do not like when each and every row has a configuration next
> to it - it does not help with transparency so much as take space up"*

- **What it means.** How a figure was worked out goes in one method note: on the tab, near
  the top, or once in the deliverable. Rows carry results, not the settings or tests behind
  them.
- **The first things it moved off rows:**
  - "alone in its band: compared with the book", once written on every such row;
  - the Test column, which named the test on every row ("z test", "exact test",
    "shuffled: N of 10,000").
- **What stays on a row:** anything that is a result for that pocket, such as its gap, its
  p-value, its dollars and its reading. Which pockets a note applies to may be listed in the
  note itself.
- **How to check a change against it.** If a column's value can be predicted from the
  settings and the pocket's size alone, it is method, not a result, and belongs in the note.
