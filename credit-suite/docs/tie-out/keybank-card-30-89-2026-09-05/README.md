## Why the numbers moved

Both changes were corrections to this exhibit, not to the data:

- **The five COULD NOTs closed.** Two capital ratios were said to have no
  single filed line to compare a percentage against — the filing publishes them
  itself, on Schedule RC-R. Three quarterly flows were said to be year-to-date
  in the filing — they are, so the quarter is the difference of two filings,
  which is the subtraction the objection describes. Each obstacle was true and
  none was a verdict.
- **Seven more fields were found to be uncheckable and then made checkable.**
  They carried the literal text `(not in tie-out map)` where their citation
  belongs, so the tie-out never reached them. 53 was never the denominator; 69
  was, and this exhibit did not say so.

## Why these files are kept

They are the first thing in this repository that put a photographed source
beside a photographed workbook cell, and the working notes that shaped
everything after it are here: the marked-up filing pages, the per-cell
screenshots, and the roster text. Deleting them would remove the record of how
the standard was arrived at, including the parts that were wrong.

Nothing in this folder should be quoted as a current figure.
